Investment underwriting · proposed MVP

Test the deal, not just the land.

Enter your own assumptions to estimate capital required, cash flow, exit proceeds, and a maximum purchase price for your target return. These calculations are illustrative and are not an appraisal or investment advice.

Transparent model. All financial assumptions below are user-entered. The downside and upside cases apply explicit stress adjustments shown under the results.

Local calculator · no account needed

Deal assumptions

Model assumptions: financing is modeled as interest-only, with the original loan principal repaid at exit. Taxes, loan fees, principal amortization, working capital, vacancies, capex after acquisition, transaction-specific legal costs and income growth are excluded unless you include them in your inputs. This simplified model is for early screening only.
